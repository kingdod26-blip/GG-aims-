How the Ahsarah story works

A small country, Ahsarah, handed its water and power to a tech megacorp. The corp's grip turned into occupation, the government fell, and a civil war started. An international task force is sent into the middle of it. This page lays out the three sides, the timeline, every character in the notes Jack sent and their ages in 2035, and who is tied to whom.

Reference only: this is TiMi Studios' world, summarised from Jack's notes and checked against public sources. Each character is marked confirmed where public sources back them up, or from notes where only Jack's notes describe them. Some backstories differ between sources (see the bottom of the page).

The premise in four steps

1 · The deal

Haavk, a global tech conglomerate, picks Ahsarah for its first big experiment: an AI system (ERAIS) that controls and shares out the whole country's water and electricity.

2 · The grip

Haavk builds up the country, then takes it over: a private security army, research into Relink brain-computer interfaces and cybernetics, and warlords paid to crush local resistance.

3 · The war

The conservative Ahsarah Guard fights back. The government collapses into open civil war. Cities split down the middle: in Brakkesh, Haavk holds the modern district and the Guard holds the old city.

4 · The third side

G.T.I., an international peacekeeping force, is deployed to stop the war, investigate Haavk and secure the infrastructure. The player is G.T.I., caught between both.

The three factions

G.T.I. · Global Threat Initiative

The peacekeepers (player)

An international coalition task force. Two generations: veterans who built it before 2030 (most now dead), and the 2035 deployment.

  • Wants: end the war, expose Haavk, secure the infrastructure.
  • Inside story: a spy (Beacon) was planted inside Haavk and killed there.
Haavk Corporation

The megacorp

Founded by Jacob Haavk. Runs Ahsarah's resources and experimental research, guarded by a mercenary security department.

  • Wants: total control of Ahsarah and its research (Relink, BCI, cybernetics).
  • Method: security forces, cybernetic constructs, funded warlords.
Ahsarah Guard

The nationalist insurgents

The rebel militia holding key sites such as Zero Dam and Longbow Valley, fighting to take their country back from Haavk and foreign forces.

  • Wants: Haavk and foreigners out; for some, the old monarchy back.
  • Fuel: the 2029 raids, which left many of its leaders orphaned.
