How the story works

Four rules hold the plot together. Each character below is built to sit on at least two of them.

1 · One place everyone needs

Ashgrove Works powers the city of Calderhaven. Whoever holds it holds the lights, the hospitals and the money. Every faction's goal is a version of "control the plant".

2 · Three sides, no clean one

Crimson X keeps order but answers to the governments that sold the plant. Halcyne Power owns it legally and abuses it. The Ash Line are the workers it was taken from, and some of them have done terrible things to get it back.

3 · The older generation's debt

On Blackout Night, 2033 a strike at the plant ended in fire. The people who died that night are why every younger character is fighting now: each of them lost someone, or caused it.

4 · Each place has a keeper

Every area of the map has a boss who runs it: the switchyard, the turbine hall, the boiler house, the cooling towers. Clearing an area means beating its keeper, and each keeper knows a piece of what happened in 2033.

5 · The agents are from here

Tariq, Blast and Silvars are not outsiders. All three worked at or near Ashgrove before 2033, which is why Crimson X sends them, and why the other two sides take it personally.

6 · The twist sits in plain sight

The man who guides you through the plant is the man who let Halcyne in. Hollis is helpful, kind and right about everything except himself.

The three factions

The arrows are what each side wants from the others. The plant sits in the middle because it is the prize.

Crimson X

The response unit

A small multinational unit set up in 2027 to secure infrastructure in a crisis. Fast, deniable, sent in at night. You play as Crimson X.

  • Wants: the plant safe and running, the city lit.
  • Weakness: its paymasters sold the plant to Halcyne.
  • Base: a forward post in the HV yard.
Halcyne Power

The owner

The energy group that bought Ashgrove in 2029. It uses the station's output to drive Project Overcurrent, a grid-scale neural network that learns to run, and to switch off, whole cities.

  • Wants: Overcurrent finished, the workers gone.
  • Weakness: Overcurrent is illegal; the evidence is inside the plant.
  • Base: the turbine hall and control room.
The Ash Line

The workers' militia

Former staff, their families and the town of Ashgrove. They seized the plant on the night the game opens and are threatening to overload it unless Halcyne is thrown out.

  • Wants: the plant back for the people who built it.
  • Weakness: a hardline wing will burn the city to make the point.
  • Base: the boiler house, coal yard and cooling towers.

Timeline

The older generation's choices up to 2033, and the younger one's war in 2041.

2027Ruth Calder founds Crimson X after a grid failure leaves a city dark for nine days. · Calder
2029The government sells Ashgrove Works to Halcyne Power. Chief Engineer Owen Hollis signs off the handover, privately promised a pension and his staff's jobs. · Halcyne, Hollis
2031Project Overcurrent starts under Dr. Imre Vasko. A third of the staff are laid off, including supervisor Kade Rusk and robotics tech Rune Halloway (Silvars). · Vasko, Rusk, Silvars
2033Blackout Night. A strike occupies the turbine hall. Halcyne's new security chief, Ashara, clears it by force; a fire starts in the boiler house. Tariq's father, Samir, dies keeping a boiler from blowing. Calder dies pulling workers out. Ashara is burned and has worn a mask since. · everyone
2034Tariq joins Crimson X. A year later Blast follows him in, then Silvars is recruited for his robots. · the agents
2038Kade Rusk organises the Ash Line out of the town's union hall. His daughter Nadia becomes its best scout. · Rusk, Nadia
2041Now. The Ash Line seizes the plant and threatens an overload. Crimson X is sent in at night to take it back. The game begins. · the campaign

Open questions for Jack

Tariq's surname

"Ziaten" is a Delta Force character's surname (Tariq Ziaten, Saeed Ziaten). Pick a new one for our Tariq. This page uses no surname until then.

Blast's real name

Drafted here as Dmitri Varga. Keep it, or give your own.

Silvars' real name

Drafted as Rune Halloway, with Silvars as his callsign. The sheet's hawk could be his own robotic bird.

Who is the final boss?

Ashara (your sheet) is drafted as the final boss, with Vasko's machine as the mid boss. Rusk could be a boss or an ally, depending on the player's choice in mission 5.

One ending or two?

Mission 5 lets you save Rusk or leave him. That could split the ending: the Ash Line runs the plant, or Crimson X hands it back to the state.

Present year

Drafted as 2041, eight years after Blackout Night. Change it and every age shifts with it.
